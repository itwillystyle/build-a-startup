=== AFTER DOWNLOAD — the three checks that have bitten this project ===

1. SCALE. 1 Blender unit = 1 Roblox stud. A Roblox character is 5 studs. Imported
   kits have landed 50x and 100x oversized before, so measure before placing.
2. ANCHOR. Imports arrive Anchored = false. Anchor before pressing Play.
3. PIVOT. The importer rotates every mesh 90 degrees about X. Reset PivotOffset to
   identity or PivotTo lays it on its side.
