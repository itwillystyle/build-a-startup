# HQ paths (the tower: Glass, Timber, Neon)
**What:** The HQ is one tower of up to 100 pieces ("levels"). Each BUILD tap adds the next piece. Since v9 (14 Oct, HQ tower 1a, branch `hq-tower`, not live until 1d) it is a rectangular glass spire with terraces: a 24-stud glass podium lobby that stands out past the tower on every side (1.4x floor 1's width: 20 studs at the sides, 8 at the front, 16 at the back; white roof band, wood columns along the front), 18-stud office floors that each step in (100 wide at floor 1 to 80 at the roof), 8 rooms per storey around a centre hall, setbacks (front, back, front) at the garden storeys 2, 6, 10, where the floor above stands on the garden deck and the step-back leaves a terrace strip (rail, planters, lamps, benches, trees) reached through a glass door in the middle room of the row behind it, a roof garden at 14 and a crown at 15. The shape lives in a pure plan, `src/ReplicatedStorage/TowerPlan.lua`. The first tap above the garage asks the player to pick a finish: Glass (saved as `W_`), Timber (`T_`) or Neon (`D_`) (`src/ServerScriptService/Wafers.lua:130-139`). Since 1d (16 Oct; spec `silicon-startup/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-16-hq-art-1d-design.md`) the finish is real art, all from Parts: every room has fins, a floor band with a 1.2-stud lip, a light line tucked under it and a faceted round corner (3 panels, radius 3); the lobby has door frames, a lit canopy and a sign board with the company's name, and planters on its roof; each terrace has a planter edge with the glass rail on it, four trees above the rail, benches and lamps; the roof has a glass crown ring with the company's name on its front and a gold beacon on the mast; the top of the shaft has a cap and the top hall a roof (no hole over the lobby); an unfinished floor has a small crane and a yellow safety rail. The three finishes are one table (`src/ReplicatedStorage/TowerFinish.lua`); the facade is the finish's one glass colour (the department colour stays on the sign and inside). The old "HQ 1-5" is a stage worked out from the level (`Wafers.lua:239`: 5, 9, 13, 18).
**How a player reaches it:**
1. Ship a product and hire one person (`src/ServerScriptService/SiliconCore.server.lua:2138-2139`).
2. Tap BUILD (rail tile, HQ pad prompt "UPGRADE" at `SiliconCore.server.lua:3058`). The card fires `WaferBuild` (`:2998`) into `tryUpgrade` (`:2251`) then `wafersBuild` (`:2130`).
3. First build only: no `s.hqPath` and level 1 or less. The server fires `AskPath` and builds nothing (`:2119-2124`). The picker (`WaferClient.client.lua`, the preview boxes from `TowerPlan.spec` at :286-292) fires `WaferPath`. The server saves the path and calls `tryUpgrade` again (`:2814-2833`).
4. The garage becomes the lobby (v9, 15 Oct, HQ part 1c; spec `silicon-startup/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-hq-garage-1c-design.md`). The BUILD that takes the plot from level 1 (`fromGarage` in `wafersBuild`, `SiliconCore.server.lua:2180`) builds without the Rise and, after the celebration branch, fires `SVRemotes.LobbyMoment` to the owner (`Wafers.lobbyMoment` `Wafers.lua:978`, payload: the new piece models, the core parts, the cab, the plot pivot). On that screen only, `LobbyMomentClient` plays about 4.4 s: scaffold up, a close shot on a copy of the garage (taken while the plot was at level 1) as it collapses flat (its shell mesh squashes to the floor; separate wall parts fold out), the lobby's columns, glass and roof rise in steps with a tick each and a sting, the scaffold drops, a low look-up, the camera back to the player; Skip at any time; the HQ orbit and the level banner do not play on this build (the `Celebrate` payload carries `lobby`); one toast from the server, "Your HQ is open! Up to N staff." It plays after each spin-off too, never on a load, a rejoin or a dev `hq:<n>`. The server has already stashed everything in the plot's `Garage` except the working parts (`TowerPlan.GARAGE_KEEP`) into `ServerStorage.GarageStash_<plot>` (`Wafers.garage` :859); `clear` (spin-off, release) brings it back. The lobby room's hall-side wall carries the garage TV and a framed "WHERE IT STARTED" photo (`lobbyArt` :504, spots `TowerPlan.WALL_ART`).
5. Stage 2 (level 5) gives the company car (`:2139-2140`). A new stage, or level 43, 68, 93 or 100, plays the level-up banner (`:2051`, `:2137-2155`).
6. The lift (v9, 15 Oct, HQ part 1b; spec `silicon-startup/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-hq-lift-1b-design.md`): one glass cab per tower rides a glass shaft in the centre hall (`TowerPlan.SHAFT`, x 5..13). Every stop has landing doors in the shaft's +Z face, a display over them (a fat floor number, then the arrow word and the room) and a call button, prompt "Lift" (`liftButton` `Wafers.lua:670`, doors and display `liftDoors` :699). The prompt calls `LiftCab.call` (`src/ServerScriptService/LiftCab.lua:309`): the button lights, the cab comes, the doors open (0.5 s). Stepping in opens the floor list; the cab's trophy panel (one socket per possible stop, lit where built, a gold flash and a star sound the first time a new one is seen) can be clicked too. A pick (`LiftCab.pick` :325, remote `SVRemotes.LiftPick`) closes the doors and rides: 1 s per floor passed, never over 4 s, a tick per floor, a louder last tick, a ding. The sign on the cab wall talks ("GOING UP!", the floor on arrival, about 1 ride in 12 a rare line). Stops are the lobby, every non-garden storey with a piece built (the one over a garden says TERRACE) and the roof garden (`LiftPlan.stops`). Landings and decks stop at the shaft (`TowerPlan.landingRects`/`deckRects`).
7. At the company's level cap the pad becomes GO PUBLIC, then spin-off (`SiliconCore.server.lua:2132-2136`).
