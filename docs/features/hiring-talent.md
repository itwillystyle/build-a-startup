# Hiring, talent, staff rigs and the Index
**What:** The HIRE pad only gives the first intern, free. Every later hire is a recruit run on the street. `hire()` in `SiliconCore` signs the person, rolls their talent once (Regular, Skilled, Star, Genius, Unicorn), builds a real R15 rig with `StaffRig`, and seats them. Rare hires get a reveal card (`TalentRevealClient`) and fill a 5 by 5 collection (`IndexClient`). `StaffAnimClient` draws all staff motion on each player's machine. Seniority (Intern to Lead) is separate from talent: it grows with seated time.
**How a player reaches it:**
1. Ship the first app, then step on the HIRE pad: "HIRE YOUR FIRST INTERN FREE" (`src/ServerScriptService/SiliconCore.server.lua:1593`, pad handler :2996-3004).
2. After that the pad reads "HIRING HAPPENS ON THE STREET" (:1568). Walk out to a candidate and use the "Recruit" prompt (`src/ServerScriptService/TalentDrop.lua:317-328`). Tiers: WALK-IN, SKILLED, STAR (HQ 2), GENIUS (HQ 3) (`RoomEconomy.lua:353-358`). The WALK-IN stands on your drive. SKILLED, STAR and GENIUS stand at a new spot each restock, 140-190, 210-270 and 290-370 walking studs from your HQ doorstep (`TalentDrop.lua:159-188`, `ChaseRules.lua:85-89`).
3. Carry them home before the timer ends while a headhunter chases (TalentDrop; see chase.md). At your doorstep (within 14 studs, `Chase.DELIVER_R`) `stepCarry` calls `hire(player, plot, {floor, fee, luck, kind})` (`TalentDrop.lua:675-683`).
4. Skilled or better: the TalentReveal card, a screen glow and a halo on Star and up. A new Index entry flies to the INDEX button (`TalentRevealClient.client.lua:258,192`).
5. INDEX button on the right rail opens the grid. Q closes it (`IndexClient.client.lua:273-287`).

**Gotchas:**
- A pinned person sits in their room only while it has a free seat; otherwise they sit as unpinned and KEEP the pin. Unpinned people can shift when a pin takes their seat: they were never promised a room.
- The `StaffAction` remote drops anything over 4 a second before looking at it, silently (no reply), so a spam burst gets 4 actions at most.
- Loyalty is clamped to the new company's cap at a spin-off (a 14 Oct decision; the spec only said "kept"), the same rule as `carried` for Skill and Speed.
- The pad refuses a second hire: "Recruit on the street" (:1915-1918). "No desks free" when `s.staff >= capacityOf` (:1920).
- Talent is `max(roll, recruit.floor)`. The tier they wore is a floor (:1933-1934). The card says "Signed from the street" for a floor, "Lucky! 1 in N" for a roll above it (`TalentRevealClient.client.lua:272`).
- Draws run rarest first so a Unicorn is exactly 1 in 1491 (:290). A Scout Report can raise talent after the roll (:1891).
- `recruit` refuses unless you own the plot, HQ meets the tier, no carry is active, you shipped, staff >= 1, a seat is free and cash >= fee (`TalentDrop.lua:591-614`). It has no distance check, so raw SVDev `recruit` starts the hunter far from you (`DevScenarios.lua:233-234`).
- Genius floor sets `s.jr.genius` for the HQ 3 task (:1890).
- The reveal waits for `WelcomeDone` up to 25 s (`TalentRevealClient.client.lua:311-314`). Right after a rejoin it can lag.
- Index keys are `role:1-5`. Load keeps only keys that match `^(%a+):(%d)$` and a known role (`SaveLoad.lua:325-327`). Spin-off does not clear `s.index`.
- A person's look comes from a seed. New hires use their staff number unless `who` is given (`SiliconCore.server.lua:1534-1538`), so a rejoin keeps faces only through `people[]`.
- Avatar templates build at server start. A hire in the first seconds waits up to 12 s, then falls back to the block rig (`StaffRig.lua:439-447,505-515`).
- Do not move motion back to the server. The header measured 246.5 KB/s per player when it was there (`StaffRig.lua:29-33`).
- A kept spin-off hire returns as an Intern (`spawnStaff` sets `tier = 1`, :1477).
- `StaffAnimClient.client.lua:47` says the run animation id is empty. `HUNTER_ANIM.run` is set (:52-53). The comment is stale.
