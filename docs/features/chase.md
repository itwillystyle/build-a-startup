# The chase (Talent Row)
**What:** Talent Row is where hires wait for you, the rarer ones farther away. You recruit one, ride a scooter home to your HQ doorstep with them, and a rival headhunter chases you. It stalks, crouches (the tell), then lunges down a line it picked. You live by steering off the line or hitting BOOST. The server owns the chase (`TalentDrop` runs `ChaseRules` at 20 Hz). The client only draws the camera, lane, sound and speed lines. The daily VIP run is the same chase with two hunters.
**How a player reaches it:**
1. Ship a product and have 1+ staff. The hire pad only gives the free first intern; every later hire is a recruit run (`SiliconCore.server.lua:1897-1913`, `TalentDrop.lua:603-604`).
2. Reach the tier's HQ level: SKILLED 1, STAR 2, GENIUS 3 (`RoomEconomy.lua:355-357`). A tier above your HQ is removed from the street (`TalentDrop.lua:826-833`).
3. Go find them. Each restock puts a chase tier's candidate at a new random spot. The spot is a WALKING distance from your doorstep inside the tier's band: SKILLED 140-190 studs, STAR 210-270, GENIUS 290-370 (`ChaseRules.lua:85-89`, `TalentDrop.lua:159-188`). Look for the light pillar (:309-312). Only the free walk-in stays on your drive (`TalentDrop.lua:190-199`). The quest guide points at the best one you can afford (`SiliconCore.server.lua:1751-1756`).
4. Hold the Recruit prompt (0.35 s, 10 studs). Only the plot owner has it enabled (`TalentDrop.lua:317-323, 596`, `TalentRowClient.client.lua:40`).
5. `recruit()` passes its checks, then `startCarry` builds the scooter, starts the offer timer and spawns the hunter (`TalentDrop.lua:549-589, 591-623`). The timer is 14 s plus 0.11 s per stud of the route home (`RoomEconomy.lua:359-360`, `TalentDrop.lua:557-558`).
6. Ride home. `ChaseCamClient` takes the camera, locks the mouse for steering, and Shift is BOOST (`ChaseCamClient.client.lua:327-335, 403-424`, `ChaseFxClient.client.lua:161-166`).
7. Get within 14 studs (`DELIVER_R`) of your doorstep. The fee is paid there through `api.hire` (`TalentDrop.lua:675-683`). You lose them if caught, if the timer ends, or if you die (`:711-714, 685-688, 669-672`). While you carry, `DropOffClient` draws the zone: a green ring at its real size (`CarryHomeR`), a light beam and a DROP OFF sign with the distance. The doorstep's height ignores every plot's HQ tower (`doorPos` `TalentDrop.lua:137`): from HQ level 18 the entrance arch spans the forecourt and the doorstep used to land on top of it, 18.4 studs up.

**Gotchas:**
- Home is your front yard: `doorPos` is (0, 66) in plot space, the open forecourt in front of the HQ entrance (`TalentDrop.lua:130`, `doorPos` :137). Not the HQ pad (inside the building, no pathfinding route) and not the entrance court (0, 30), which is inside the Wafers ground floor behind a doorway under 12 studs wide (no path leaves it, so every candidate fell back to the drive; measured 10 Oct). Until 10 Oct it was (-73, 70), the old layout's door line, unmarked and 73 studs off to the side: his recording shows him riding in through the front door and being caught inside his own HQ.
- Why v4.6 exists: delivery used to be stepping onto the lot, and the lot covers the first 175 studs of the drive. A SKILLED candidate stood inside it (a 0-stud chase). A STAR candidate stood 18 studs out, inside the old 45-stud home stretch (a star hunter held at 7.1 studs for 12 s). Now the home stretch is `HOME_SAFE` 20 and candidates stand 140-370 walking studs out (`ChaseRules.lua:74-89`).
- `exploreSpot` picks a random point, 12 tries, and keeps one only if the point is flat street-level ground (normal Y > 0.9, within 10 studs of the door height, not water, not in any lot) and the PathfindingService route from the door is inside the band (`TalentDrop.lua:168-186`). If no try passes, the candidate stands on the old drive spot and has no `PathLen` (:279-288). Then the offer timer uses the straight line (:557). Each spawn can cost up to 12 route calls, so DevRecorder waits 9 s after a restock.
- The hunter spawns `START_BEHIND` 22 studs from the candidate, AWAY from your door (`TalentDrop.lua:582-586`). The old +X offset put it between you and home. `recruit()` still has no distance check, only the prompt's 10 studs (`:321`). A raw recruit from far away makes a hunter far from you. The scenarios teleport first (`DevScenarios.lua:233-234`).
- A lunge starts only from behind (70 degrees) a moving player. A hunter that cut a corner and is now beside you waits. Without this rule, skilled straight runners were caught 113 of 400 times, all on the last 30 studs (`ChaseRules.lua:90-96`). Freezing is still punished: under 2 studs/s the hunter can lunge again 2 s after the last lunge (:98-102).
- The crouch paces you but stops at `HOLD` 7. Pacing at your speed used to walk the hunter into a player stuck on a kerb (caught in the crouch with no lunge, live) (`ChaseRules.lua:239-249`).
- The sim and the map must agree. The sim's route is `bandMid - 40` studs of road plus a 40-stud turn, so it equals the band middle (`chase.spec.luau:19, 133-142`). The sim is still a straight road with one turn. The live route is a pathfinding path to a random spot. Unclear how well escape % matches live play.
- The fee is checked at pickup but charged at the door. The carry can fail there if cash fell or the seat went (`TalentDrop.lua:610, 679-682`).
- Every pickup of a chase tier spawns a hunter. There is no chance roll (`TalentDrop.lua:577-587`). The reveal, intercept and random-chase ideas in the 8 Oct threat spec (Phase 2) are not built.
- Only the VIP gets a second hunter. `ChaseRules.lua:117` says "GENIUS / VIP", but genius has `hunters = 1` (:143) and the spawn needs `c.vip` (`TalentDrop.lua:694`).
- Hunters move in 20 Hz steps (`TalentDrop.lua:855-868`). `HunterSmoothClient` draws them about 50 ms behind (`HunterSmoothClient.client.lua:11-15`). A jump over 8 studs or a 0.25 s gap skips smoothing (:22-23, :65). The `ChaseRules.lua:3` header says "every server frame". Code says 20 Hz.
- The lane: `LungeFrom` and `LungeTo` are written once when the crouch starts and cleared after the lunge (`TalentDrop.lua:720-735`). `ChaseFxClient` paints it 9 studs wide, two catch radii (:42, :271-289).
- Camera yaw is the player's yaw. A shot may change distance, height, side, lens and roll, never facing, because W is camera-relative (`ChaseCam.lua:413-416`).
